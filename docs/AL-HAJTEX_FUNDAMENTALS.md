# AL-HAJTEX: fundamental report

Al-Haj Textile Mills Limited (DSE code AL-HAJTEX, ISIN BD0401ALHAJ8). Written 2026-11-05.

This describes the company. It is not a forecast or advice. Each figure gives its source and date. **[unverified]**
marks a figure that came from a secondary site or that I could not confirm against a company filing.
"DSE news" means the company's own disclosures as listed on `https://www.dse.com.bd/company/AL-HAJTEX`, read on
2026-11-05 with this repo's `dse_quant.watch.news()`.

## In short

- **A small, old cotton-yarn spinner that is losing money.** The audited loss per share was Tk 1.64 in FY25 and
  Tk 2.85 in FY26 (years end 30 June). The core business also lost money in FY24 (an operating loss of Tk 81 mn). That
  year's reported profit came from a one-off gain on a court case.
- **The price is far above book value.** At Tk 68.70 (5 Nov 2026) the share trades at about **5.2x** the FY26 NAV per
  share of Tk 13.15. The five textile peers checked trade at 0.4x to 1.5x. P/E cannot be computed because earnings are
  negative.
- **Cash keeps leaving the business.** Operating cash flow per share has been negative in every year shown here
  (FY22 to FY26). In FY26 it was −Tk 6.45, about Tk 144 mn in total.
- **There is a large legal and governance problem.** A loan case filed by Agrani Bank in 2013 is still open. Both
  recent audit reports draw attention to it. Results for FY23 to FY25 were filed one to two years late. In Feb 2026
  BSEC rejected a proposed 35% stock dividend, citing "inconsistencies in the audited financial statements".
- **The share fell 49% in a year** (Tk 133.5 to Tk 68.2, 4 Nov 2025 to 4 Nov 2026), while the DSEX rose 6% and most
  textile peers rose. Institutions cut their holding from 12.75% to 1.07% over the same period.

## 1. Business

- Incorporated in **1962** as a private limited company. It is one of Bangladesh's oldest cotton-yarn makers. It was
  nationalised in 1971, returned to its owners in 1982, and listed on the DSE in **1983** (TBS, 10 Dec 2020; DSE
  company page, 5 Nov 2026).
- **Product:** cotton yarn. The single factory is at I.K. Road, Ishurdi, Pabna. The head office is at 66 Dilkusha C/A,
  Dhaka (DSE company page). I found no published spindle count or capacity figure **[unverified]**.
- **Operating history:** the factory closed for two years more than a decade ago because the company could not open
  letters of credit for cotton imports. It was laid off again from June 2019, citing weak yarn demand, unsold stock
  and blocked working capital (FE, 11 Aug 2019; TBS, 10 Dec 2020). In May 2023 TBS reported the machinery was about
  30 years old and that over Tk 17 crore of yarn sat unsold because the board had not approved the sale (TBS, 7 May
  2023).
- **Reinvestment since 2025:** in Jul 2025 the board approved buying two used Schlafhorst Autoconer 338 machines for
  Tk 4.8 mn (DSE news, 17 Jul 2025). In Feb 2026 it approved an extra Tk 78 mn for BMRE (balancing, modernisation,
  rehabilitation and expansion) at the factory (DSE news, 19 Feb 2026).
- **Shares:** 22,298,549 shares of Tk 10 face value, so paid-up capital is Tk 222.99 mn. Authorised capital is
  Tk 500 mn. The category is **B**. The company was moved to Z on 30 Mar 2026 for paying its dividend late, then back
  to B on 31 Mar 2026 (DSE company page; DSE news, 30–31 Mar 2026).

## 5. Debt and cash flow

**Debt** at 30 Jun 2025 (FY25 audit notes 14 and 22, published by DSE with the Emphasis of Matter on 23 Aug 2026):

- Long-term loan fund from Agrani Bank: **Tk 216.70 mn**, down from Tk 236.29 mn a year earlier after a Tk 50 mn
  payment. It is made up of principal of Tk 36.81 mn and accrued interest of Tk 179.89 mn.
- Bank overdraft (cash credit) with Agrani Bank: **Tk 18.77 mn**.
- The DSE company page shows the same two figures, but with no date.
- The company's land, buildings and machinery are mortgaged to Agrani Bank (note 14).
- The bank claims much more than the company's books show. In its 2013 case it claimed Tk 367.5 mn plus interest,
  against book balances of Tk 139.1 mn and Tk 18.8 mn (FY23 audit, note 14.01). Bank statements from 2011–12 also
  show balances above the company's figures.

**Inventory** at 30 Jun 2025 was Tk 202.4 mn (FY25 audit, note 5). Of that, **Tk 159.0 mn was finished goods**,
about half a year's sales at the unverified FY25 revenue figure.

**Operating cash flow per share (NOCFPS), in Tk:** FY22 (3.66), FY23 (3.23), FY24 (2.58), FY25 (3.76), **FY26 (6.45)**
(DSE news). That is five years of operating cash outflow, and FY26 was the largest at about Tk 144 mn. I could not
find a cash balance, so I cannot say how long current cash lasts **[unverified]**. The outflows have most likely been
covered by the money received from Agrani Bank, but that is an inference.

## 6. The Agrani Bank dispute (main legal risk)

The sources below describe this at different dates, and their figures do not fully reconcile.

- The company kept a fund with Agrani Bank that by 2008 had become an FDR (fixed deposit) of about Tk 249.5 mn. The
  bank offered to pay only Tk 50.8 mn. The company sued (writ 5129/2009) and won in the High Court (2011) and the
  Appellate Division (2014, 2019). By 2019 it had received **Tk 439.5 mn** in total (FY23 audit, note 14.01).
- The bank, for its part, filed a loan-default case in 2013 (Artha Rin Adalat case 89/2013, Pabna). That case is
  **still unresolved**, according to the FY25 auditor (report published by DSE, 23 Aug 2026).
- Settlement talks in Dec 2022 reportedly cut the payable to Tk 382 mn. A later letter put it at Tk 526 mn (TBS,
  7 May 2023). Neither appears as settled in the FY25 accounts.
- **What this means for the figures:** the FY24 profit and NAV rely on treating the bank money as income. If the bank
  wins more than the company has booked, the company would have to record a larger liability, and NAV would fall.

## 7. Governance and reporting

- **Late filing:** FY23 results were filed Dec 2024, FY24 in Dec 2025 and FY25 in Jul 2026. The rule is four months
  after year-end. The FY25 auditors were only appointed on 8 Feb 2026 and could not attend the year-end stock count
  (FY25 Emphasis of Matter). FY26 results (28 Oct 2026) were on time.
- **Board conflict:** BSEC rebuilt the board in 2022. In 2023 the MD and the chairman were in open dispute (TBS,
  7 May 2023). The FY23 audit report was issued unsigned until "directors of two group" signed it (FY23 audit,
  Other Information).
- **BSEC actions:** it rejected the 35% stock dividend on 22 Feb 2026. Margin loans for buying the share were barred
  from 30 Mar 2026 under rule 11(8) of the Margin Rules 2025 (DSE news). I did not check whether that bar still
  applies.
- **Auditor's opinion:** unmodified for FY23 and FY25, with Emphasis of Matter paragraphs in both. I did not see the
  FY24 or FY26 audit opinions **[unverified]**.
- **Unusual price moves:** DSE queried the company in Feb 2025 and Sep 2025. Both times the company said there was no
  undisclosed price-sensitive information (DSE news).

## 11. Main risks

1. **Continuing losses and cash burn.** There have been two straight years of losses, getting larger, and five years
   of operating cash outflow. The FY24 profit was a one-off.
2. **The Agrani Bank case (89/2013).** The size of the liability is disputed and the collateral is the whole factory.
   An adverse ruling could reduce NAV.
3. **Reliability of the accounts.** Filings were up to two years late, auditors were appointed late, BSEC cited
   "inconsistencies", and the NAV figures in different filings disagree.
4. **Valuation compared with the business.** The price is 5x book for a loss-making company, and peers trade near or
   below book. Almost no institutions hold the share now.
5. **Old plant and unsold stock.** The machinery is about 30 years old and finished-goods stock was Tk 159 mn. BMRE
   spending (Tk 78 mn approved) has not yet shown up in the results.
6. **Market and regulatory restrictions.** It was briefly in Z category in 2026, has a margin-loan bar, and has a
   history of price-surge queries. With a free float of about Tk 969 mn and around Tk 13 mn of daily turnover, the
   share can move sharply.
